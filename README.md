## Architecture Overview

* **Layer 1 (Hero WebGL Shader)**: Cursor-color-shifting fluid simulation in raw GLSL with `#ifdef GL_FRAGMENT_PRECISION_HIGH` cross-GPU mobile support.
* **Layer 2 (3D Sand Sculpture)**: 3,800 advecting particles across 26 interwoven filaments with dynamic velocity color-shifting (24K Gold `#D4AF37` ↔ Emerald Growth `#10B981`).
* **Layer 3 (Halftone Wordmark)**: Interactive procedural dot-grid shockwave canvas responding to pointer physics.
* **Speed-to-Lead Engine**: Interactive 4.4x ROAS Simulator (supporting INR, USD, EUR, and AED) paired with a Dual-Dispatch Configurator form executing asynchronous `POST /api/leads` persistence and instant WhatsApp dispatch (`+91 7678439995`).
* **Hardware Tiering & Viewport Culling**: Heuristic device detection (Tiers 1–3) with IntersectionObserver culling that halts 60 FPS physics loops when sections scroll out of view.
